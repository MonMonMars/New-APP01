## 1. Inherent risk

Online dating and meeting strangers carry inherent risks including harassment, fraud, assault, and theft. **You assume all risks** associated with your use of Spark and your interactions with other members, on or off the platform.

---

## 2. Verification is not a safety guarantee

Photo, liveness, and age verification badges confirm certain checks passed at a point in time. They are **not**:

- Criminal background checks  
- Guarantees of character, intentions, or safety  
- Proof that someone is safe to meet alone  

Always use your own judgment.

---

## 3. Meeting in person

- Meet in **public**, busy places for first dates  
- Tell a friend your plans and location  
- Arrange your own transport  
- Use **Date check-in** in chat to share where you are meeting  
- Leave immediately if you feel uncomfortable  
- Do not share financial details, passwords, or home address early  

See our Safety Center in-app for more tips.

---

## 4. Report & block

Report suspicious behaviour via profile or chat menu → **Report**. Block removes someone from your deck and chats immediately.

**Emergencies:** Contact local emergency services first. Then email support@spark.app.
