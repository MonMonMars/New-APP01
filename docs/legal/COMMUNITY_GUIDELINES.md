# Spark — Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** 15 September 2025  
**Last updated:** 14 September 2026

Spark is built for **respectful, honest connections**. These guidelines apply to all members in **Spark** and **Pulse** disguise mode. Violations may result in content removal, feature restrictions, or permanent ban.

---

## 1. Be respectful

- Treat others as you would in person.  
- No harassment, hate speech, slurs, or attacks based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.  
- No threats, intimidation, or encouraging self-harm or violence.  
- Accept “no” gracefully. Unwanted contact after a block or unmatch is not allowed.

---

## 2. Be honest

- Use recent photos of **yourself**. No catfishing or impersonation.  
- Do not lie about your age, relationship status, or identity to manipulate others.  
- Verification badges must reflect **your** completed checks — do not fake or borrow badges.

---

## 3. Keep it safe

- **18+ only.** No accounts for minors.  
- No nudity, sexually explicit content, or solicitation in public profile areas where prohibited.  
- No sharing of others’ private images without consent (“revenge porn”).  
- No promotion of illegal drugs, weapons, or unlawful activity.  
- No financial scams, crypto schemes, or requests for money from people you have not met in person.

**Meeting in person:** Use public places, tell a friend, and trust your instincts. See Safety Center in the app.

---

## 4. No spam or manipulation

- No mass messaging, bot behaviour, or automated scraping.  
- No advertising unrelated products/services without permission.  
- No fake engagement (fake likes, coordinated reporting, review manipulation).

---

## 5. Disguise mode (Pulse) — extra rules

Pulse lets you browse dating content in a news-style feed for privacy in public spaces. **Disguise is not permission to break the rules.**

You must **not**:

- Use disguise mode to stalk, harass, or deceive matches  
- Pretend disguised profile cards are real news articles or official ads to third parties  
- Use the feature to evade law enforcement or legal process  
- Display other people’s photos without their consent in disguise creatives  

See [Disguise Mode Policy](./DISGUISE_MODE_POLICY.md).

---

## 6. Reporting and blocking

- **Report** profiles or messages that violate these guidelines (menu → Report).  
- **Block** anyone you do not want contact from.  
- We review reports and may take action without notifying the reporter of outcomes.

False or abusive reporting may itself result in action against your account.

---

## 7. Enforcement

Actions we may take include:

| Severity | Examples | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Warning | Minor first offence | Warning + content removal |
| Restriction | Repeated issues | Limited messaging, shadowban, loss of boost |
| Suspension | Serious or repeated violations | Temporary account freeze |
| Permanent ban | Illegal content, threats, underage, fraud | Account termination + possible law enforcement referral |

We are not obligated to provide advance notice in cases involving imminent harm or illegal activity.

---

## 8. Appeals

Email support@spark.app with “Guidelines appeal” and your account email. We aim to review within a reasonable time. Decisions are final where required by safety or law.
