# Spark / Pulse — Legal documents

**Last updated:** 14 September 2026

This folder contains template legal policies for the **Spark** dating app and **Pulse** disguise mode. They are designed to reduce common product-liability, privacy, and user-conduct risks for a mobile dating service.

---

## ⚠️ Important — not a substitute for a lawyer

These documents are **starting templates** for product design and engineering. They are **not legal advice**. Before a public launch, you should:

1. Engage qualified counsel in **each jurisdiction** where you operate (e.g. Hong Kong, Taiwan, EU/UK, United States).
2. Replace all `[PLACEHOLDER]` fields with your real company name, address, and contact details.
3. Register as a data controller / file privacy notices where required (e.g. PDPO, GDPR, CCPA).
4. Review app-store requirements (Apple App Store, Google Play) for dating apps, age gates, and in-app purchase terms.

## Recommended user flows

1. **Onboarding** — Require explicit acceptance of Terms + Privacy Policy (checkbox + links) before account creation.
2. **Disguise mode** — First-time Pulse activation should show a short summary of the Disguise Mode Policy.
3. **Verification** — Link to Trust & Verification Policy before biometric capture.
4. **Safety Center** — Keep all policies one tap away (implemented in-app).
5. **Material changes** — Email + in-app notice 30 days before substantive ToS/Privacy changes where practicable.
