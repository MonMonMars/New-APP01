# Spark — Pulse Disguise Mode Policy

**Effective date:** 15 September 2025  
**Last updated:** 14 September 2026  
**Applies to:** Pulse disguise mode within Spark (the “**Disguise Feature**”)

---

## 1. What disguise mode is

**Pulse** is an optional interface that presents Spark dating activity as a generic news and social feed (headlines, sponsored cards, comment-style posts). This helps protect your privacy when using the app in public.

Disguise mode:

- **Does not** delete or hide your data from Spark’s systems  
- **Does not** make you anonymous to matches you have already connected with  
- **Does not** change your obligations under our [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) or [Community Guidelines](./COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md)  
- **Is intended** for personal privacy convenience, not deception of law enforcement or other users for unlawful purposes  

---

## 2. How it works

| Element | Behaviour |
|---------|-----------|
| **Default landing** | After onboarding, Pulse may be the default view |
| **Toggle** | Emergency / Pulse logo switches between Pulse and Spark |
| **Feed content** | Mix of real-style news/ad/social cards + disguised dating profiles |
| **Profile cards** | Dating profiles appear as news, ad, or social-style cards with masked avatars |
| **Owner cues** | Close reading shows labels (e.g. “Profile”) visible to **you**, the account owner |
| **AI disguise** | Optional tool to generate overlay images from your photos for disguise creatives |

---

## 3. Your responsibilities

By enabling disguise mode, you agree that:

1. **You remain accountable** for all dating activity on your account, whether viewed in Spark or Pulse.  
2. **You will not** use disguise mode to harass, stalk, scam, or mislead others.  
3. **You will not** represent disguised cards as genuine third-party journalism or advertising to people who are not the account owner.  
4. **You understand** that people glancing at your screen may still infer you are using a social app.  
5. **You own or have rights** to photos used in disguise creatives.  
6. **Demo builds** may use sample news branding (BBC, The Verge, etc.) for illustration — we are not affiliated with those publishers unless stated.

---

## 4. Third-party appearance

Disguise mode may **resemble** news apps or social feeds. This is a **visual design pattern** for privacy. Unless explicitly stated:

- We are **not** affiliated with news organisations, brands, or advertisers shown in demo content  
- Headlines and articles may link to real external sites; we do not control third-party content  
- Sponsored cards in demo builds are **sample ads**, not endorsements  

Do not use disguise mode to infringe trademarks, pass yourself off as a media outlet, or commit fraud.

---

## 5. Data and disguise mode

We store:

- Your disguise on/off preference (e.g. local device storage)  
- Settings for AI-generated disguise overlays  
- Standard dating data (profile, matches, messages) regardless of which UI you use  

See [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for full details.

---

## 6. Safety limitations

Disguise mode **does not**:

- Guarantee that others cannot identify you  
- Prevent screenshots or screen recording by third parties  
- Replace blocking, reporting, or verification tools  
- Protect you from malware or physical device access  

Always lock your device and use OS-level screen privacy where available.

---

## 7. Revocation

We may disable disguise mode for accounts that misuse it or where required by law. Continued access to Spark may still be available in standard mode.
