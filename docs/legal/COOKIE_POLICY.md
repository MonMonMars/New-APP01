# Spark — Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** 15 September 2025  
**Last updated:** 17 September 2026  
**Operator:** Spark Labs Ltd.  
**Applies to:** Spark web application (spark.app and related domains)

---

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser. We also use local storage and similar technologies for session state, preferences, and consent records.

---

## 2. Essential cookies

Required to run Spark on web:

| Purpose | Examples |
|---------|----------|
| Authentication | Login session, security tokens |
| App state | Disguise mode, onboarding progress |
| Consent | Cookie and privacy preference storage |

These cannot be disabled while using the Service.

---

## 3. Analytics cookies (optional)

With your consent we use anonymised analytics to understand usage, fix bugs, and improve performance. You can choose **Essential only** in the cookie banner or disable analytics in **Settings → Privacy controls**.

---

## 4. Personalisation

When enabled, we may store preferences to rank profiles and content. This is separate from marketing and can be toggled in Privacy controls.

---

## 5. Third-party cookies

We minimise third-party cookies. Payment flows may use store or payment-provider cookies subject to their policies. We do not use third-party advertising trackers on Spark web.

---

## 6. How to manage cookies

- Use the cookie banner on first visit  
- Change preferences in **Settings → Privacy controls**  
- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings  

Blocking essential cookies may prevent Spark web from working.

---

## 7. Retention

Session cookies expire when you close the browser. Consent records persist until you withdraw consent or clear site data. Analytics cookies typically expire within 13 months.
