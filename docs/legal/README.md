## User agreement flows (implemented)

1. **Onboarding** — Checkbox accepts Terms, Privacy, Community Guidelines, Disguise Mode Policy, and Safety Disclaimer.
2. **Disguise mode** — First-time Pulse activation shows Disguise Policy modal summary.
3. **Verification** — Checkbox accepts Trust & Verification Policy before biometric capture.
4. **Web cookies** — Banner with Cookie Policy + Privacy Policy links; Essential only / Accept.
5. **Purchases** — Spark+ and consumables link to Subscription Terms at confirm.
6. **Safety Center & Privacy controls** — All policies one tap away.
